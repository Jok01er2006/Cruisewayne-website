ABOUT CRUISE WAYNE

The artist behind the music.

MY NAME IS CRUISE WAYNE

I am an independent Nigerian Afrobeats recording artist and a member of FG Gang.

My musical journey officially started on 20 June 2025. My inspiration comes from life's struggles, pain, suffering, personal experiences and the influence of my friends.

I believe music is more than entertainment. It is a way to communicate emotions, tell stories and connect with people.

Through my music, I want to reach people who understand life's challenges and appreciate the strength it takes to keep moving forward.

Artist: Cruise Wayne
